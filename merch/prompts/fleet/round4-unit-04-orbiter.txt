Locked-off POV shot from an engineering camera mounted on a relay satellite in low lunar orbit. Foreground: the satellite's own gold multilayer-insulation foil and the edge of a dark solar panel, rigid and perfectly steady, catching slow-moving glints of harsh sunlight. Middle distance: a companion relay satellite of the same constellation holding formation, rigid body, station-keeping. Below, the grey cratered lunar surface slides past slowly and steadily, and this orbital parallax carries the only large motion in the frame. Hard vacuum stillness, harsh raking light, long stark shadows, inky black sky. Documentary space-engineering footage, muted desaturated grade, subtle sensor noise, slight vignette.
